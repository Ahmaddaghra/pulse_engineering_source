# Unresolved assumptions and required evidence — v0.2

All entries below are **OPEN**. Accepting the specification does not establish these premises or pass G0–G5. Roles are responsibilities to assign; no named person's signoff is claimed. Evidence and decisions must be versioned with the relevant gate record before the blocked capability can proceed.

| ID | Unresolved premise / decision | Responsible role | Required evidence / resolution | Blocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| A02 | Authorized feeds can provide sufficiently complete and timely first-observation windows | Backend/data owner | Prospective freshness, rollover, outage/recovery, parse/queue latency and clock-quality measurements; freeze completeness/healthy-owner rules and permitted polling budget | G0/G1 and scorable G4/G5 windows |
